❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Char Dham 2026, Helicopter & Taxi FAQs

Essential guidelines on opening dates, biometric passes, IRCTC helicopter regulations, satvik meals, and transparent taxi tariffs.

The sacred portals of Yamunotri and Gangotri traditionally open on the auspicious occasion of Akshaya Tritiya (early May 2026). Kedarnath Dham opens shortly after (determined on Maha Shivratri), and Sri Badrinath Dham opens on Basant Panchami announcement. The yatra remains open through autumn till Diwali / Bhai Dooj in November 2026.

Mandatory Biometric Registration: The Government of Uttarakhand mandates biometric registration and Yatra QR passes for every pilgrim via registration.uk.gov.in. When you travel with Jai Maa Tour & Travels, our Haridwar operations staff handles registration for your entire family/group free of charge.

Best months to visit: May to June for warm spring weather, and September to October for crystal-clear Himalayan skies, uncrowded temples, and panoramic snow peaks.

Kedarnath helicopter flights operate from helipads situated in Phata, Sersi, and Guptkashi. Tickets are officially governed by UCADA and booked through the IRCTC Heli Yatra portal.

Weight & Baggage Regulations: Standard tickets cover passengers weighing up to 80 kg. Any weight above 80 kg incurs an overweight surcharge per kg at the helipad weighing scale. The maximum permissible passenger weight is 120 kg. Luggage is strictly restricted to one soft handbag (2 to 5 kg) per passenger.

Medical Fitness Advisory: Kedarnath is at an altitude of 3,583 meters (11,755 feet). Atmospheric oxygen levels are noticeably lower. Yatris with a history of cardiovascular, respiratory, or hypertension ailments must consult their physician and carry portable oxygen canisters, which our drivers also have on standby.

Himalayan weather can change rapidly. Jai Maa Tour & Travels maintains a 24x7 Operations Command Room in Haridwar directly tuned into the Uttarakhand State Disaster Management Authority (USDMA), Border Roads Organisation (BRO), and local police traffic control.

1. Advance Payment Terms

All bookings require a 25% token advance to block vehicle commercial road permits, allocate verified mountain drivers, and confirm hotel room allocations. 50% payment is payable upon arrival in Haridwar or Dehradun prior to starting the tour. 2. Transparent Cancellation Refund Schedule

  • 30 Days or More Prior: 90% refund of token advance (10% administrative processing deduction).

  • 7 to 14 Days Prior: 50% refund of advance payment.
  • Under 7 Days / Force Majeure: 100% token advance is transferred to a 12-Month Open Rescheduling Credit Voucher, allowing you to reschedule anytime without penalty.

3. Emergency Weather & Landslide Shelter Guarantee

Mountain weather in Uttarakhand can encounter brief government-mandated travel pauses. Under our safety guarantee, our drivers stay stationed with guests. Emergency overnight stays are arranged at nearest safe GMVN or private partner facilities at government-subsidized rates. No pilgrim is left unattended on mountain highways.

4. Free Biometric Yatra Registration Concierge

Under Uttarakhand state regulations, biometric registration is mandatory for Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, and Yamunotri. Our Haridwar desk issues your biometric registration QR passes free of charge.
