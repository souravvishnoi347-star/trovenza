Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Haridwar / Rishikesh to Chopta Alpine Basecamp

06:30 AM – 03:00 PM (200 km / ~7 hrs drive)

Depart Haridwar early morning along the holy Ganga and Alaknanda rivers. Stop to marvel at the divine Devprayag Sangam. Drive through Rudraprayag and the dense Kedarnath Sanctuary forest up into Chopta (2,680 m). Check into your cozy alpine Swiss tent or mountain cottage. Enjoy hot evening tea, witness sunset over the snowline, and gather for dinner around a warm campfire.

🍴 Warm Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Chopta Alpine Swiss Camp / Wooden Cottage
Day 2

Chopta to Tungnath & Chandrashila Peak Summit Push

05:30 AM – 03:30 PM (5 km ascent each way / ~6-7 hrs trek)

Wake up to the crisp mountain breeze and a nourishing breakfast. Begin your ascent along the well-paved stone trail to Tungnath Temple (12,073 ft). Offer prayers at the sanctum sanctorum of the third Kedar. Continue your final 1.5 km climb to the Chandrashila Summit (13,123 ft). Revel in a spellbinding 360-degree panorama of Chaukhamba, Trishul, Nanda Devi, Kedarnath, and Thalaysagar. Descend comfortably back to basecamp for celebratory dinner.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Chopta Swiss Alpine Camp / Sari Village Resort
Day 3

Chopta to Haridwar / Rishikesh via Optional Deoria Tal Hike

07:30 AM – 04:30 PM (Return Drive)

After breakfast, take an optional 2.5 km morning hike to the serene Deoria Tal lake (reflecting the towering Chaukhamba peaks in its crystal waters) or commence the scenic drive back to Rishikesh / Haridwar. Drop at the railway station by late afternoon.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Haridwar / Rishikesh

🍽️ About Your Meal Plan (Modified American Plan - MAP)

Includes hearty hot breakfasts and multi-course dinners with fresh chapati, dal, seasonal sabzi, paneer, rice, and hot gulab jamun/kheer. Energy snacks and hot tea provided before summit climb.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ Can beginners do the Tungnath Chandrashila trek in 2N/3D?
Yes, thousands of first-time trekkers complete this route every year. The trail is paved up to Tungnath and our guide sets a comfortable walking pace.
❓ Are mobile networks and electricity available at Chopta camps?
Airtel and Jio offer decent connectivity at Chopta. Swiss camps provide solar lighting and power generator backup in the evenings.
