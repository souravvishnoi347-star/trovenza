Comprehensive Schedule

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Delhi / Haridwar to Rishikesh – Sacred Ganga & Cafe Trails

06:00 AM Pickup – 01:00 PM Check-in

Depart early morning from Delhi NCR or meet at Haridwar. Arrive in Rishikesh and check into your boutique resort. Post lunch, indulge in optional river rafting or explore the vibrant cafes of Tapovan overlooking the turquoise Ganga. At dusk, witness the divine Aarti at Triveni Ghat as hundreds of floating earthen diyas illuminate the sacred river.

🍴 Warm Buffet Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Rishikesh Boutique Valley Resort
Day 2

Rishikesh to Mussoorie – Kempty Falls & Mall Road Sunset

08:30 AM – 04:00 PM (85 km / ~2.5 hrs drive)

Enjoy breakfast and drive towards Mussoorie ("Queen of Hills"). Visit the famous Kempty Falls for mountain splashes and photography. Check into your hotel and take the cable car ropeway up to Gun Hill for views of the Doon Valley. Spend the evening strolling along the Mall Road, tasting local street delicacies and shopping for hand-knitted woolen shawls.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Mussoorie Mountain-View Hotel
Day 3

Landour Heritage Tour & Return Journey to Delhi / Haridwar

08:30 AM – 06:00 PM (Return Drive)

After breakfast, drive up to the British-era cantonment of Landour. Visit Sister’s Bazaar, stop at the historic Char Dukan, taste fresh lemon cake at Landour Bakehouse, and capture 360-degree views from Lal Tibba. Descend via Dehradun back to Haridwar Railway Station or Delhi NCR, arriving refreshed and recharged.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Delhi / Haridwar

🍽️ About Your Meal Plan (Modified American Plan - MAP)

Our MAP plan includes delicious breakfast buffets (parathas, puris, eggs to order, toast, tea/coffee) and rich dinners with paneer specialties, seasonal vegetables, dal, rice, tandoori rotis, and desserts. Tailored satvik or jain meals are available without onion and garlic.
