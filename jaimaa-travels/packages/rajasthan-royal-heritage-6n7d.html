Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Pink City Jaipur Arrival – Amber Fort & Jal Mahal

Welcome to Rajasthan. Chauffeur reception at Jaipur International Airport. Visit the monumental Amber Fort with its sparkling Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace). Photo stop at Jal Mahal floating on Man Sagar Lake. Check in and visit Birla Mandir.

🍽️ Royal Dinner Included 🏨 Jaipur Heritage Haveli
DAY 2

City Palace, Jantar Mantar & Drive to Sun City Jodhpur

Morning tour of City Palace museum and the astronomical instruments of Jantar Mantar (UNESCO site). Drive 5 hours across desert highways to the blue city of Jodhpur. Check into your heritage hotel.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Jodhpur Heritage Hotel
DAY 3

Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada & Drive to Jaisalmer

Tour the colossal Mehrangarh Fort perched on a cliff 400 feet high, with preserved cannons and royal palanquins. Visit the white marble cenotaph Jaswant Thada. Scenic drive across Thar desert sands to Jaisalmer (The Golden City).

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Jaisalmer Desert Resort
DAY 4

Jaisalmer Fort & Thar Desert Camel Safari in Sam Dunes

Explore Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Qila) where people still reside inside stone ramparts, and ornate merchant havelis. In the afternoon, head to Sam Sand Dunes. Enjoy a camel safari over sweeping dunes, Rajasthani folk dances, and stay in luxury Swiss tents.

🍽️ Breakfast & Desert Camp Feast ⛺ Sam Sand Dunes Swiss Tent
DAY 5

Jaisalmer to Udaipur via 1444-Pillared Ranakpur Temple

Depart desert dunes and drive towards the City of Lakes Udaipur. En route, tour the 15th-century Ranakpur Jain Temple, famous for its 1,444 uniquely carved marble pillars. Arrive in romantic Udaipur and check into your lake resort.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Udaipur Lake-Facing Resort
DAY 6

Udaipur City Palace & Lake Pichola Luxury Boat Cruise

Tour Rajasthan's largest royal palace complex: Udaipur City Palace, Saheliyon Ki Bari fountain gardens, and Jagdish Temple. In the evening, take a private boat cruise on Lake Pichola watching Jag Mandir and Lake Palace glow at sunset.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Udaipur Lake-Facing Resort
DAY 7

Udaipur Old Bazaar Stroll & Airport Departure

Savor a leisurely royal breakfast. Shop for miniature paintings, bandhani sarees, and silver jewelry before transfer to Udaipur Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) for your onward journey.

🍽️ Breakfast Included ✈️ Flight Departure
