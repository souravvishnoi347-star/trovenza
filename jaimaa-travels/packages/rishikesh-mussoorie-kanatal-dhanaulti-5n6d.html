Comprehensive Schedule

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Rishikesh – River Rafting & Divine Triveni Aarti

10:00 AM – 07:30 PM (Adventure & Culture)

Arrive at Dehradun Airport or Haridwar Railway Station and transfer to your riverside camp/resort in Rishikesh. Gear up with certified lifejackets and helmets for an exhilarating 16 km White Water Rafting expedition from Shivpuri through Roller Coaster and Golf Course rapids. In the evening, witness the soulful Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat with Vedic chants and synchronized brass lamps.

🍴 Warm Buffet Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Rishikesh Riverside Resort / Luxury Boutique Hotel
Day 2

Rishikesh Spiritual Trail, Beatles Ashram & Neer Garh Waterfall

08:30 AM – 05:00 PM (Heritage & Nature)

Begin with morning yoga or riverside walk followed by breakfast. Visit the legendary Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia) covered in vibrant graffiti art where the band composed the White Album. Walk across the suspension footbridges, explore Gita Bhawan, and take a short hike to the pristine Neer Garh Waterfall for refreshing mountain spring water dips.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Rishikesh Riverside Resort
Day 3

Rishikesh to Mussoorie via Dehradun (Robber’s Cave)

09:00 AM – 03:00 PM (85 km / ~3 hrs drive)

Post breakfast, drive up into the foothills of the Garhwal Himalayas. En route, stop at Dehradun’s Robber’s Cave (Guchhupani) where cold subterranean streams flow inside narrow limestone gorges. Ascend to Mussoorie ("Queen of Hills"). Check in to your hotel and spend the evening shopping along the bustling Mall Road and Kulri Bazaar.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Mussoorie Mountain-View Hotel
Day 4

Mussoorie Exploration: Kempty Falls, Company Garden & Landour

09:00 AM – 05:30 PM (Sightseeing & Heritage)

Visit the cascading Kempty Falls and enjoy lunch at charming hill cafes. Tour the Company Garden with its floral nursery and amusement lake. Head up to Landour cantonment - visit Char Dukan, taste English bakes at Landour Bakehouse, and view the high snowline from Lal Tibba (highest point of Mussoorie). Return for sunset at Camel’s Back Road.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Mussoorie Mountain-View Hotel
Day 5

Mussoorie to Dhanaulti & Kanatal Ridge

09:00 AM – 03:00 PM (45 km / ~2 hrs scenic drive)

Drive along the pine-fringed Mussoorie-Chamba ridge to Dhanaulti. Explore the twin Eco Parks (Amber and Dhara) nestled among virgin Deodar forests. Continue to the revered Surkanda Devi Temple (take the scenic cable car up to 9,995 ft). Arrive at your tranquil resort in Kanatal. Evening bonfire, music, and stargazing in pollution-free Himalayan night skies.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Kanatal Alpine Resort / Swiss Luxury Camp
Day 6

Kanatal to Dehradun / Haridwar Departure

08:30 AM – 02:00 PM (Scenic Return Drive)

Enjoy a peaceful mountain breakfast with fresh valley air. Drive down via Chamba and the Tehri bypass to Dehradun Airport or Haridwar Railway Station for your onward return journey, filled with memories of adrenaline and tranquility.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Dehradun / Haridwar

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ Is river rafting safe for beginners and non-swimmers?
Yes, absolutely. The Shivpuri to Rishikesh 16 km stretch has Grade II & III rapids which are safe for non-swimmers under the supervision of IRF-certified river guides with mandatory safety lifejackets.
❓ Can this package be customized for pickup from Delhi NCR?
Yes, we provide doorstep pickup from Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, or IGI Airport in our private sanitized vehicles at nominal additional cost.
❓ What is the temperature like in Kanatal and Dhanaulti?
In summer (April-June), temperatures range between 15°C and 25°C, making it blissfully cool. In winter (Dec-Feb), temperatures hover between -2°C and 10°C with frequent snowfall.
