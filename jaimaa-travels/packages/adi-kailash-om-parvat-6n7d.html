Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Kathgodam to Pithoragarh via Kainchi Dham & Almora

06:30 AM – 04:30 PM (190 km / ~7-8 hrs scenic hill drive)

Depart early from Kathgodam. Stop for divine darshan at Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj’s Kainchi Dham. Drive through Almora and scenic pine valleys to Pithoragarh ("Mini Kashmir of Uttarakhand"). Check into your hotel and enjoy views of the Soar Valley.

🍴 Warm Multi-Course Vegetarian Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Pithoragarh Deluxe Hotel
Day 2

Pithoragarh to Dharchula (Inner Line Permit & Medical Verification)

08:30 AM – 02:00 PM (95 km / ~3.5 hrs drive)

Drive along the Kali River border to Dharchula, the gateway town. Submit physical biometric documentation at SDM office for Inner Line Permits (ILP) and complete basic medical fitness checkups. Visit the Nepal hanging bridge across the Kali river for cross-border cultural exploration.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Dharchula Valley Hotel
Day 3

Dharchula to Gunji / Nabi Village via Chialekh (4x4 Mountain Expedition)

06:00 AM – 03:00 PM (75 km / ~5 hrs rugged 4x4 drive)

Board high-clearance 4x4 Bolero Campers. Drive past majestic waterfalls at Malpa and Budi, ascending through the hairpin bends of Chialekh. Cross the border checkpoint and enter the serene Vyas Valley with snow-clad peaks. Check into traditional homestays in Gunji or Nabi, decorated with traditional Rung ethnic paintings.

🍴 Breakfast & Warm Satvik Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Nabi / Gunji Traditional Alpine Homestay
Day 4

Gunji to Jyolingkong, Parvati Sarovar & Sacred Adi Kailash Darshan

05:30 AM – 04:00 PM (34 km drive + gentle walk to lake)

Set out at dawn towards Jyolingkong. Behold the majestic pyramidal peak of Mount Adi Kailash (19,646 ft) towering above. Walk or take a pony to the pristine Parvati Sarovar (14,990 ft). Perform sacred prayers at the Shiva-Parvati Temple on the lake shore and view Gauri Kund. The mirror reflection of Adi Kailash in the turquoise lake water is a spiritual experience of a lifetime. Return to Nabi for dinner.

🍴 Breakfast & Hot Satvik Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Nabi / Gunji Alpine Homestay
Day 5

Gunji to Nabidang (Om Parvat Miraculous Darshan) to Dharchula

05:30 AM – 05:00 PM (High Altitude Excursion & Descent)

Drive early to Nabidang facing the sacred Om Parvat (18,340 ft). Gaze upon the miraculous phenomenon where dark rock ridges combine with virgin white snow to form a distinct, perpetual ॐ (OM) symbol. Offer prayers to Lord Shiva. Begin your descent in 4x4 campers back to Dharchula for a restful evening.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Dharchula Hotel
Day 6

Dharchula to Chaukori via Patal Bhuvaneshwar Cave Temple

08:00 AM – 04:00 PM (135 km / ~5.5 hrs drive)

Drive to the mystical subterranean limestone cave shrine of Patal Bhuvaneshwar. Walk inside the ancient caves through iron chains to witness stalagmite and stalactite formations depicting the 33 crore Hindu deities. Continue to the tea-garden town of Chaukori facing the Nanda Devi range.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Chaukori Mountain Resort
Day 7

Chaukori to Kathgodam / Pantnagar Departure

08:00 AM – 04:00 PM (175 km / ~6 hrs drive)

Enjoy morning sunrise over the Panchachuli peaks. Drive down via Almora and Bhowali to Kathgodam Railway Station or Pantnagar Airport with heartfelt spiritual bliss.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Departure Drop at Kathgodam / Pantnagar

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ Is a passport or special permit required for Adi Kailash and Om Parvat?
Indian citizens require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) issued by the SDM Dharchula, along with a police verification certificate and basic medical fitness report. We handle the complete documentation and permit procurement on your behalf.
❓ How tough is the road from Dharchula to Gunji?
Thanks to the newly constructed BRO highway, the route is now motorable by 4x4 high-clearance vehicles. Walking is only required for the short gentle lake stretch at Parvati Sarovar.
❓ What is the best age group for this yatra?
Pilgrims aged 10 to 70 with reasonable physical health can undertake this sacred tour. Oxygen cylinders are kept in our backup vehicles for acclimatization.
