Everything You Need to Know

❓ Why get married at Triyuginarayan Temple?
Triyuginarayan is the sacred spot where Lord Shiva married Goddess Parvati in the presence of Lord Vishnu and Lord Brahma during the Satya Yuga. The Akhand Dhuni (eternal sacred fire) that witnessed their holy wedding has burned continuously ever since. Tying the knot around this eternal flame is believed to grant lifelong marital bliss.
❓ What is included in the ₹4,00,000 package?
Everything you need for a stress-free celestial wedding for 30 guests: 15 resort rooms, all meals (breakfast, lunch, high tea, royal dinners), dedicated luxury travel from Haridwar, Haldi setup, Mehndi setup, Sangeet stage & sound, Temple Mandap decoration, bridal makeup artist, cinematic photography & videography, temple priest fees, and event management.
❓ Can more guests be added beyond 30?
Yes, easily! Additional guests can be accommodated at ₹5,500 per person including extra twin-sharing luxury rooms, all meals, and ceremony access.
❓ How are temple wedding permissions and rituals handled?
Our on-site event team liaises directly with the local temple management and head priests of Triyuginarayan. We coordinate the auspicious muhurat, puja samagri, and seating for your 30 guests.
