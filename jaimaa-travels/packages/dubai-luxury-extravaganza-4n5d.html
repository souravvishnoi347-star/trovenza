Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Arrival in Dubai – Marina Luxury Dhow Cruise with Dinner

Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Meet our executive representative and transfer to your 4-star downtown hotel. In the evening, board a traditional Arabian wooden Dhow at Dubai Marina. Enjoy an international buffet dinner while cruising past illuminated illuminated glass skyscrapers and luxury yachts.

🍽️ Buffet Dinner on Cruise 🏨 4-Star Downtown Hotel
DAY 2

Dubai City Tour, Burj Khalifa 124th Floor & Dubai Mall Fountains

Morning panoramic city tour covering Dubai Frame, Zabeel Palace, Jumeirah Beach with Burj Al Arab photo stop. In the afternoon, explore the massive Dubai Mall and ascend to the 124th & 125th floors of the Burj Khalifa for 360-degree skyline views. Witness the dancing Dubai Fountain show.

🍽️ Breakfast Included 🏨 4-Star Downtown Hotel
DAY 3

Morning Souk Leisure & Thrilling Red Dunes 4x4 Safari with BBQ

Morning visit to Dubai Gold & Spice Souks across Dubai Creek on an authentic Abra boat. At 3 PM, luxury 4x4 Land Cruisers pick you up for heart-thumping dune bashing in the Lahbab desert. Enjoy sunset photography, sandboarding, camel rides, shisha, and a lavish BBQ dinner with live entertainment.

🍽️ Breakfast & Desert BBQ Dinner 🏨 4-Star Downtown Hotel
DAY 4

Full-Day Abu Dhabi Tour: Sheikh Zayed Mosque & BAPS Mandir

Full-day tour to UAE's capital Abu Dhabi. Visit the awe-inspiring Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque with pure white Macedonian marble and the world's largest hand-knotted carpet. Tour the architectural marvel BAPS Hindu Mandir, Corniche drive, and Ferrari World photo stop.

🍽️ Breakfast Included 🏨 4-Star Downtown Hotel
DAY 5

Morning Duty Free Shopping & Airport Departure

Savor a leisurely buffet breakfast. Last-minute luxury shopping at Mall of the Emirates or Dubai Mall. Private luxury transfer to Dubai International Airport for your flight back home.

🍽️ Breakfast Included ✈️ Flight Departure
