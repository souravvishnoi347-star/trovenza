Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Arrival in Holy Ayodhya – Shri Ram Janmabhoomi & Saryu Aarti

Arrive at Ayodhya Maharishi Valmiki International Airport or Ayodhya Dham Railway Station. Welcome by private chauffeur and hotel check-in. In the afternoon, visit the magnificent Ram Mandir for VIP Darshan of Ram Lalla. Evening serene boat ride on the sacred Saryu River and witness the grand Saryu Aarti.

🍽️ Satvik Dinner Included 🏨 4-Star Ayodhya Hotel
DAY 2

Hanumangarhi, Kanak Bhawan & Drive to Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)

Morning blessings at Hanumangarhi Temple and the gold-adorned Kanak Bhawan. Drive 3.5 hours across the historic Awadh plains to Prayagraj. Check in, and take a private boat to the holy Triveni Sangam for a sacred dip where Ganga, Yamuna, and mythical Saraswati unite. Visit Anand Bhawan and Bade Hanuman Ji (reclining Hanuman temple).

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Prayagraj Heritage Hotel
DAY 3

Prayagraj to Kashi – Dashashwamedh Ghat Grand Ganga Aarti

After breakfast, drive 2.5 hours to Varanasi (Kashi), the world's oldest living city. Check in, rest, and head to Dashashwamedh Ghat at dusk. Experience the world-renowned Ganga Aarti from a reserved riverboat as brass lamps illuminate the holy waters and conch shells reverberate.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Varanasi Riverside Hotel
DAY 4

Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan, Kal Bhairav & Sarnath Buddhist Stupa

Early morning VIP Darshan at the sacred Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple and Annapurna Temple via the newly built corridor. Visit Kal Bhairav (Kotwal of Varanasi). Afternoon excursion to Sarnath where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon; explore Dhamek Stupa and the Ashoka Pillar museum.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Varanasi Riverside Hotel
DAY 5

Subah-e-Banaras Sunrise Boat Ride & Departure

Witness the magical Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat ride floating past Manikarnika, Harishchandra, and Assi Ghats. Savor authentic Banarasi Kachori and Malaiyo. Transfer to Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Cantt Railway Station for your return journey.

🍽️ Breakfast Included ✈️ Departure Transfer
