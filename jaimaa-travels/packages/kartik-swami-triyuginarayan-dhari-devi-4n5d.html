Comprehensive Schedule

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Haridwar to Srinagar / Rudraprayag via Devprayag & Dhari Devi Darshan

07:00 AM – 04:00 PM (165 km / ~5-6 hrs drive)

Depart Haridwar along the scenic Badrinath National Highway. Witness the confluence of Bhagirathi and Alaknanda at Devprayag. Stop at Kalyasaur for heartfelt darshan of Maa Dhari Devi - the fierce protector goddess whose deity was miraculously preserved on the river. Continue to Rudraprayag (confluence of Alaknanda and Mandakini). Check into your riverside hotel.

🍴 Warm Buffet Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Rudraprayag / Srinagar Riverside Hotel
Day 2

Rudraprayag to Triyuginarayan Temple & Guptkashi

08:00 AM – 03:30 PM (75 km / ~3 hrs drive)

After breakfast, ascend along the Mandakini valley past Agastyamuni and Guptkashi to the sacred village of Triyuginarayan. Enter the centuries-old stone temple where Lord Shiva married Goddess Parvati in the presence of Lord Vishnu. Offer wood samagri into the Akhand Dhuni (perpetual holy flame) and collect sacred holy ash. Visit Saraswati Kund and Brahm Kund. Overnight in Guptkashi.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Guptkashi Mountain Resort
Day 3

Guptkashi to Kanakchauri & Kartik Swami Sunset Ridge Trek

09:00 AM – 05:00 PM (45 km drive + 3 km scenic trek)

Drive through quiet rhododendron ridges towards Kanakchauri, the base hamlet for Kartik Swami. Embark on a gentle 3 km stone trail along a narrow Himalayan ridge flanked by thousands of temple bells. Reach the ridge-top Kartik Swami Temple (10,000 ft) dedicated to Lord Murugan. Experience an electrifying sunset with 360-degree views of Chaukhamba and Bandarpoonch ranges.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Kanakchauri Scenic Camp / Rudraprayag Hill Hotel
Day 4

Sunrise Ridge View to Ukhimath Omkareshwar Temple

06:00 AM – 03:00 PM (Heritage & Spirituality)

Catch early morning golden sunrise over the Greater Himalayas. Descend to base and drive to Ukhimath to visit the historic Omkareshwar Temple - the winter seat where the Rawal priests perform daily worship of Lord Kedarnath and Madhyamaheshwar during winter months. Relax in the peaceful spiritual atmosphere.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Ukhimath / Chopta Foothill Resort
Day 5

Ukhimath to Rishikesh / Haridwar Return with Vashistha Cave Stop

08:00 AM – 04:30 PM (185 km / ~6 hrs drive)

Post breakfast, drive down along the Ganga. Stop at the ancient Sage Vashistha Cave (Vashistha Gufa) on the white sandy banks of the Ganga for 20 minutes of serene silent meditation. Drop off at Rishikesh or Haridwar Railway Station.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Haridwar / Rishikesh

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ Is Kartik Swami trek difficult?
Not at all. The Kartik Swami trek is a well-paved 3 km trail with gradual stairs and railings on the final ridge. It takes around 1.5 hours to climb up and is suitable for all age groups.
❓ Can couples perform special puja at Triyuginarayan Temple during this trip?
Yes! Couples frequently visit Triyuginarayan to take blessings for a prosperous married life and offer wood to the Akhand Dhuni. Our driver will assist you with local temple priests.
