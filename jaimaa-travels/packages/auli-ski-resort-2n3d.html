Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Haridwar / Rishikesh to Joshimath – Gateway to Auli

06:00 AM – 04:00 PM (275 km / ~8-9 hrs scenic hill drive)

Depart Haridwar early morning along the holy Alaknanda. Stop at Devprayag, Rudraprayag, and Karnaprayag confluences. Ascend through Chamoli to the ancient mountain town of Joshimath. Check into your hotel, enjoy evening tea, and visit the sacred Shankaracharya Math and the legendary Kalpavriksha tree.

🍴 Warm Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Joshimath Valley Hotel / Auli Scenic Resort
Day 2

Joshimath to Auli Snow Paradise & Gorson Bugyal Trek

08:30 AM – 05:00 PM (Full Day Alpine Excursion)

Board the iconic Joshimath to Auli cable car ropeway gliding over snow-capped slopes. Walk along the glistening Auli lake, participate in fun skiing/snowboarding activities, and take the chairlift to Tower 10. Embark on a gradual 3 km trek to the sweeping alpine meadow of Gorson Bugyal with unobstructed views of Nanda Devi, Kamet, and Dunagiri. Watch the alpine glow at sunset before heading to dinner.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Joshimath / Auli Mountain Resort
Day 3

Joshimath to Haridwar / Rishikesh Return Journey

07:00 AM – 04:30 PM (275 km / ~8 hrs drive)

Enjoy your morning breakfast amidst mountain views. Board your private vehicle and journey down the scenic highway with a brief stop at Dhari Devi Temple or Devprayag. Arrive at Haridwar / Rishikesh Railway Station by evening.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Haridwar / Rishikesh

Includes delicious breakfast buffets (parathas, puris, eggs to order, toast, tea/coffee) and rich dinners with paneer specialties, seasonal vegetables, dal, rice, tandoori rotis, and desserts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ When is the best time for skiing in Auli?
The best time for skiing and winter sports in Auli is from mid-December through the end of February when the slopes are blanketed with fresh powder snow.
❓ Is the Auli Ropeway operational throughout the year?
Yes, the cable car operates year-round (weather permitting), offering panoramic aerial views of the Himalayas in all four seasons.
