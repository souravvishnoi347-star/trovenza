Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Arrival in Kathmandu – The Valley of Gods

Welcome to Nepal! Reception at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) with a traditional Rudraksha garland. Chauffeur transfer to your hotel. Rest or take a leisurely evening walk through the vibrant cafes and trekking shops of Thamel.

🍽️ Dinner Included 🏨 Kathmandu 4-Star Hotel
DAY 2

Sacred Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath & Swayambhunath

Morning VIP Darshan at Pashupatinath Temple, one of the supreme Shiva temples on Earth. Continue to Boudhanath Stupa, center of Tibetan Buddhism. In the afternoon, ascend Swayambhunath for 360-degree vistas of Kathmandu Valley.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Kathmandu 4-Star Hotel
DAY 3

Scenic Mountain Highway to Pokhara – Lake City of Nepal

Drive 6 hours along the Trishuli River highway (optional river rafting stop) or 25-minute scenic flight to Pokhara (2,700 ft). Arrive at Pokhara and check into your lakeside resort. Evening relaxing boat ride on Phewa Lake.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Pokhara Lakeside Resort
DAY 4

Sarangkot Annapurna Sunrise, Davis Falls & Gupteshwor Cave

Early morning drive to Sarangkot to witness the breathtaking golden sunrise over Mount Dhaulagiri, Annapurna I-IV, and Machapuchare. Return for breakfast, then tour Davis Fall, the subterranean Gupteshwor Cave, and the Peace Pagoda.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Pokhara Lakeside Resort
DAY 5

Pokhara to Kathmandu – Durbar Square & Souvenir Shopping

Scenic journey back to Kathmandu. Visit Kathmandu Durbar Square or Patan Durbar Square to admire Newari pagoda architecture, ancient stone statues, and royal courtyards. Shop for authentic singing bowls and thangka paintings.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Kathmandu 4-Star Hotel
DAY 6

Farewell Nepal & Flight Departure

Relish your final Himalayan breakfast with mountain views. Chauffeur transfer to Kathmandu Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight back home with fond memories.

🍽️ Breakfast Included ✈️ Airport Transfer
