Comprehensive Schedule

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Kathgodam / Delhi Arrival to Nainital – The Lake Capital

Morning Pickup – 02:00 PM Check-in

Meet our executive chauffeur at Kathgodam Railway Station (or Delhi airport/hotel on request). Enjoy a refreshing 1.5-hour hill drive passing tall oak and rhododendron forests to Nainital. Check into your lake-facing or ridge hotel. In the evening, take a leisurely paddle or yacht boat ride on Naini Lake, visit Maa Naina Devi Temple, and stroll along the lively Mall Road and Tibetan Bazaar for local candle shopping.

🍴 Warm Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Nainital Premium Valley/Lake View Hotel
Day 2

Nainital Lake Tour: Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal & Snow View

09:00 AM – 05:00 PM (Full Day Sightseeing)

After breakfast, venture into the captivating Kumaon Lake District. Visit Bhimtal with its picturesque island cafe in the lake center, Naukuchiatal with its nine scenic corners, and Sattal, a cluster of seven interconnected freshwater lakes fringed by thick pine woods. In the afternoon, return to Nainital for a cable car / ropeway ride up to Snow View Point for an initial glimpse of the snowline.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Nainital Premium Hotel
Day 3

Nainital to Almora via Sacred Kainchi Dham (Neem Karoli Baba)

08:30 AM – 03:00 PM (65 km / ~3.5 hrs drive)

Check out after breakfast and drive towards Almora. En route, make a sacred stop at the globally celebrated Kainchi Dham Ashram of Baba Neem Karoli Ji. Spend peaceful moments meditating by the river stream. Continue to Almora, the cultural heart of Kumaon. Check in and visit Chitai Golu Devta Temple - famous for thousands of brass temple bells - and witness a dramatic sunset at Bright End Corner.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Almora Heritage Hill Resort
Day 4

Almora to Kausani via Kasar Devi & Katarmal Sun Temple

09:00 AM – 02:30 PM (52 km / ~3 hrs drive)

Visit Kasar Devi Temple, situated along the Earth’s Van Allen radiation belt (revered for intense cosmic meditative energy, frequented by Swami Vivekananda and Bob Dylan). Drive past the 9th-century Katarmal Sun Temple before arriving at Kausani - dubbed the "Switzerland of India" by Mahatma Gandhi. Check into your resort with private balconies opening to a 300 km panoramic Himalayan snow wall.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Kausani Panorama Himalayan Resort
Day 5

Kausani Sunrise, Tea Gardens & Ancient Baijnath Temple

06:00 AM – 04:00 PM (Local Sightseeing & Heritage)

Awake to a golden sunrise over Trishul (7,120m), Nanda Devi, and Panchachuli. Visit Anasakti Ashram where Mahatma Gandhi penned his commentary on the Bhagavad Gita. Stroll through the lush Kausani Tea Estate and sample aromatic orthodox teas. In the afternoon, descend to the historic town of Baijnath to admire the 12th-century stone temple dedicated to Lord Shiva on the banks of Gomti river.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Kausani Panorama Himalayan Resort
Day 6

Kausani to Kathgodam Departure via Ranikhet

08:30 AM – 03:30 PM (140 km / ~5-6 hrs drive)

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and begin your return drive via Ranikhet ("Queen’s Meadow"). Visit the famous Chaubatia Apple Orchards, the Kumaon Regimental Centre Memorial, and the high-altitude Army Golf Course. Drop at Kathgodam Railway Station or continue to Delhi for your onward voyage.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Drop-off at Kathgodam / Delhi

🍽️ About Your Meal Plan (Modified American Plan - MAP)

Your MAP package includes daily chef-crafted morning breakfasts and elaborate buffet dinners at each destination. Dinners present hot regional Kumaoni specialties (such as Bhatt ki Churkani or Aloo ke Gutke upon request) alongside North Indian curries, dal tadka, rotis, rice, and sweet dishes. Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ Can we visit Kainchi Dham Ashram comfortably during this trip?
Yes, Kainchi Dham is situated right on the highway between Nainital and Almora. Our itinerary dedicates ample time for darshan, meditation, and prasad at Baba Neem Karoli Ji’s Ashram.
❓ Which railway station is closest for this package?
Kathgodam Railway Station (KGM) is the closest railhead, just 35 km from Nainital. We provide seamless pickup and drop from Kathgodam. Delhi pickup/drop is also available.
❓ Is this package suitable for senior citizens and children?
Yes! This is one of Uttarakhand’s most comfortable leisure circuits with smooth paved roads, moderate altitudes, scenic resort stays, and minimal strenuous walking.
❓ Can we see snow during this tour?
In winter (mid-December to February), Nainital and Kausani frequently receive snow. During all seasons, the snow-covered peaks of Trishul and Nanda Devi remain clearly visible from Kausani.
