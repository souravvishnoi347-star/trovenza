Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Haridwar / Dehradun to Barkot via Kempty Falls Mussoorie

Meet our pilgrimage manager at Haridwar Railway Station. Scenic drive ascending through Mussoorie and stopping at Kempty Falls. Continue through Yamuna Valley to Barkot (4,200 ft). Check into your mountain resort with views of Bandarpoonch peak.

🍽️ Satvik Dinner Included 🏨 Barkot Mountain Resort
DAY 2

Barkot to Yamunotri Dham Darshan & Sacred Surya Kund

Drive to Janki Chatti and commence the 6 km trek (or pony/doli) to holy Yamunotri Dham. Take a holy bath in hot sulfur springs of Jamunabai Kund, cook rice prasadam in Surya Kund, and receive blessings of Goddess Yamuna. Trek back to Janki Chatti and return to Barkot.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Barkot Mountain Resort
DAY 3

Barkot to Uttarkashi – Kashi Vishwanath Temple Darshan

Drive 4 hours along the Bhagirathi River to Uttarkashi (3,800 ft), known as the Kashi of the Himalayas. Visit the ancient Kashi Vishwanath Temple and the legendary 6-meter-tall divine Shakti Trishul.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Uttarkashi Riverside Hotel
DAY 4

Uttarkashi to Gangotri Dham & Submerged Shivling

Scenic drive through Harsil Valley apple orchards. Arrive at holy Gangotri Dham (10,200 ft). Take a holy dip in the crystal Bhagirathi River, attend the Ganga Puja in the white granite temple, and view the submerged rock Shivling. Return to Uttarkashi.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Uttarkashi Riverside Hotel
DAY 5

Uttarkashi to Guptkashi / Sitapur via Mandakini Valley

Drive 7 hours across Tehri Dam reservoir and through Tilwara to Guptkashi / Sitapur in the Mandakini valley. Check in and prepare for the sacred Kedarnath darshan with medical and helipad clearances.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Guptkashi / Sitapur Cottage
DAY 6

Guptkashi to Kedarnath Dham – Trek or Helicopter & Evening Aarti

Early transfer to Sonprayag/Gaurikund for the 16 km trek, or board your scheduled helicopter flight from Phata/Sersi. Arrive at holy Kedarnath (11,755 ft). Attend the divine Sandhya Aarti as temple bells echo against the icy Kedar Dome.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Kedarnath Shrine Cottage / Camp
DAY 7

Morning Kedarnath Darshan, Trek Down & Drive to Pipalkoti

Perform morning Rudrabhishek in the 8th-century temple. Descend to Gaurikund or fly down to Phata. Drive through Chopta / Chamoli to Pipalkoti or Joshimath for an overnight valley rest.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Pipalkoti Mountain Hotel
DAY 8

Drive to Sri Badrinath Dham, Tapt Kund Snan & Evening Aarti

Ascend past Joshimath and Vishnuprayag to Sri Badrinath Dham (10,279 ft). Take a holy bath in the natural hot springs of Tapt Kund. Enter the colorful temple for Lord Badri Vishal darshan. Evening Swarna Aarti.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Badrinath Deluxe Hotel
DAY 9

Mana Village (Last Indian Village) & Drive to Rudraprayag

Visit Mana Village: Vyas Gufa where Mahabharata was dictated, Ganesh Gufa, Bhim Pul over the roaring Saraswati River. Afternoon scenic descent past Nandaprayag and Karnaprayag to Rudraprayag.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Rudraprayag Riverside Resort
DAY 10

Devprayag Sangam & Rishikesh Ganga Aarti – Haridwar Drop

Visit the holy confluence of Bhagirathi and Alaknanda at Devprayag to see the birth of Mother Ganga. Stop at Rishikesh Ram Jhula and Triveni Ghat before your evening drop-off at Haridwar Railway Station.

🍽️ Satvik Breakfast Included 🚗 Departure Convoy
