Comprehensive Schedule

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Haridwar / Rishikesh to Joshimath via Devprayag & Rudraprayag

06:00 AM – 04:30 PM (275 km / ~8-9 hrs scenic hill drive)

Begin your journey from Haridwar/Rishikesh along the winding Alaknanda river. Stop at the holy Devprayag Sangam to witness Bhagirathi and Alaknanda uniting to form the Holy Ganga. Drive past Srinagar, Rudraprayag, and Karnaprayag with lunch stop at a river-view mountain dhaba. Arrive at Joshimath (gateway to Badrinath and Auli) by late afternoon. Check into your hotel, enjoy evening hot tea, and acclimatize.

🍴 Dinner included at Joshimath hotel (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Joshimath Premium Valley-View Hotel
Day 2

Joshimath to Auli Snow Paradise & Gorson Bugyal Meadow Trek

08:00 AM – 05:00 PM (Ropeway / Mountain Cab + 3 km trek)

After a lavish hot breakfast, board Asia’s longest aerial cable car (Ropeway) or drive up to Auli. Revel in the breathtaking white slopes, visit the man-made artificial lake, and undertake an easy 3 km scenic trek to Gorson Bugyal through fragrant oak and deodar forests. Stand face-to-face with India’s 2nd highest peak, Mount Nanda Devi (7,816 m), along with Kamet, Dunagiri, and Hathi-Ghoda peaks. Enjoy snow fun/skiing trials (winter) before returning to Joshimath for an evening stroll at Jyotirmath.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Joshimath / Auli View Hotel
Day 3

Joshimath to Chopta Meadows via Chamoli & Mandal Forest

09:00 AM – 02:00 PM (95 km / ~3-4 hrs drive)

Relish morning breakfast and embark on a picturesque journey through the untouched Mandal Valley and dense Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary. Wind through rhododendron groves to Chopta (2,680m), popularly revered as the "Mini Switzerland of India". Check into your alpine swiss tent or boutique cottage nestled amidst pine woods. Enjoy an evening tea with views of snow-capped peaks, followed by a cozy Himalayan bonfire under a starlit canopy.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Chopta Swiss Alpine Camp / Boutique Cottage
Day 4

Chopta to Tungnath Mahadev & Chandrashila Peak Summit Trek

05:30 AM – 03:00 PM (4.5 km ascent each way / ~6-7 hrs trek)

Start early with an energizing morning meal to catch magical sunrise hues. Begin the stone-paved trek from Chopta base to Tungnath Temple (12,073 ft) - the highest Shiva shrine on Earth with over 1000 years of history. After divine darshan, push further 1.5 km to the summit of Chandrashila Peak (13,123 ft). Experience an awe-inspiring 360-degree amphitheater view of Chaukhamba, Trishul, Kedar Dome, and Gangotri ranges. Descend comfortably back to Chopta camp for evening relaxation.

🍴 Breakfast & Multi-Course Dinner (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Chopta Swiss Alpine Camp / Sari Village Resort
Day 5

Chopta to Haridwar / Rishikesh via Dhari Devi Temple Darshan

07:30 AM – 04:30 PM (210 km / ~7-8 hrs drive)

Enjoy your final morning breakfast in the mountains. Board your private sanitized vehicle and commence the return drive along the Alaknanda. Stop at the legendary Maa Dhari Devi Temple suspended right over the Alaknanda river at Kalyasaur for auspicious blessings. Continue towards Rishikesh / Haridwar, arriving by late afternoon with unforgettable memories of the Himalayas.

🍴 Breakfast included (MAP Plan) 🏨 Overnight: Departure Drop at Haridwar / Rishikesh

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

❓ What is the fitness level required for Tungnath and Chandrashila trek?
The trek from Chopta to Tungnath is 3.5 km on a paved stone trail, and the climb from Tungnath to Chandrashila is 1.5 km on mountain terrain. It is categorized as easy to moderate. Anyone with basic walking fitness can complete it. Mules and ponies are also available from Chopta up to Tungnath.
❓ When does it snow in Chopta and Auli?
Snowfall typically begins in late December and lasts through mid-March. During this time, Auli operates ski courses and Chopta transforms into a picturesque white winter paradise with snow trekking.
❓ What is included in the MAP Meal Plan?
The MAP plan includes wholesome morning breakfast and sumptuous evening dinner at your hotel/camp every day. Lunch is kept flexible so you can enjoy local mountain cafes and dhabas during your day sightseeing.
❓ Which vehicle will be provided?
For 2-4 travelers: Dedicated Swift Dzire / Amaze sedan. For 4-6 travelers: Spacious Ertiga or Innova Crysta. For groups of 7-12: Luxury 12/17-seater Force Tempo Traveller. All vehicles have expert mountain chauffeurs.
❓ How do I confirm my booking and what is the payment schedule?
You can book instantly via WhatsApp or phone with a 25% advance token. The remaining balance can be cleared upon arrival in Haridwar/Rishikesh before commencing the journey.
