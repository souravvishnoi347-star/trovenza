Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Delhi / Chandigarh to Shimla – Colonial Queen of Hills

Pickup from Delhi Airport or Chandigarh Railway Station. Enjoy a winding mountain climb through the Shivalik foothills to Shimla (7,200 ft). Check into your hotel. Evening walk along the famous Ridge, Lakkar Bazaar, and Mall Road.

🍽️ Dinner Included 🏨 Shimla Valley Hotel
DAY 2

Shimla Kufri Snow View, Himalayan Nature Park & Jakhoo Temple

Post breakfast, excursion to Kufri. Enjoy yak rides, visit the Himalayan Wildlife Zoo, and view snow peaks from Mahasu Peak. Later, visit Jakhoo Hill, home to the towering 108-foot Lord Hanuman statue overlooking the town.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Shimla Valley Hotel
DAY 3

Scenic Drive from Shimla to Manali via Pandoh Dam & Kullu

A breathtaking 7-hour drive descending to Mandi and tracing the Beas River up to Kullu Valley. Stop at Pandoh Dam, Hanogi Mata Temple, and authentic Kullu handloom shawl weaving centers. Arrive in Manali (6,725 ft) and check in.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Manali Mountain Resort
DAY 4

Manali Local Sightseeing – Hadimba, Vashisht & Mall Road

Explore the ancient wooden Hadimba Devi Temple nestled inside a cedar forest, the Manu Temple, Tibetan Monasteries, and Vashisht Village with natural hot springs. Evening leisure at Mall Road for cafe hopping.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Manali Mountain Resort
DAY 5

Solang Valley Thrill & Atal Tunnel to Sissu (Lahaul)

Full-day excursion to Solang Valley. Indulge in paragliding, zorbing, and quad biking. Drive through the historic 9.02 km Atal Tunnel to cross into the barren, breathtaking landscape of Sissu in Lahaul Valley and marvel at Sissu Waterfall.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Manali Mountain Resort
DAY 6

Beas River Rafting in Kullu & Drive to Chandigarh / Delhi

Check out after breakfast. Stop for white water river rafting on the Beas River at Kullu before your scenic highway return drive to Chandigarh or Delhi for your onward flight or train.

🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Return Transfer
