Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Haridwar / Dehradun to Guptkashi / Phata Valley Drive

Meet our executive driver at Haridwar Railway Station or Dehradun Airport. Embark on a scenic drive along the holy Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers, passing Devprayag Sangam and Rudraprayag. Arrive at Guptkashi/Phata, check into your luxury valley-view cottage, and undergo the mandatory biometric Yatra QR pass check and helicopter briefing.

🍽️ Dinner Included 🏨 Guptkashi Valley Cottage
DAY 2

Helicopter Flight to Holy Kedarnath Temple & VIP Darshan

Early morning transfer to the helipad. Board your scheduled 8-minute scenic chopper ride soaring over deep mountain gorges directly to the Kedarnath helipad (11,755 ft). Walk just 500 meters to the 8th-century stone temple. Perform sacred Rudrabhishek with Sugam Darshan access. Soak in the spiritual vibrations of Kedar Valley surrounded by the snow-capped Kedar Dome. Evening flight back to Phata.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Phata / Guptkashi Resort
DAY 3

Morning Blessings & Scenic Return Drive to Haridwar / Dehradun

After breakfast, enjoy a peaceful morning amidst Himalayan mist. Drive back towards Haridwar/Dehradun, stopping at Dhari Devi Siddhapeeth on the riverbank for darshan. Drop-off at Haridwar Railway Station or Jolly Grant Airport with divine blessings of Baba Kedar.

🍽️ Breakfast Included 🚗 Departure Convoy
