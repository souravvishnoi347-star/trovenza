Detailed Journey

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Srinagar Airport Arrival – Dal Lake Shikara & Luxury Houseboat

Arrive at Srinagar Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport. Chauffeur reception and transfer to your luxury cedar houseboat docked on Dal Lake or Nigeen Lake. In the evening, enjoy a sunset Shikara ride gliding past floating gardens and lotus wetlands. Relish a warm cup of traditional Kashmiri Kahwa.

🍽️ Dinner Included 🛶 Dal Lake Luxury Houseboat
DAY 2

Srinagar to Meadow of Flowers Gulmarg – Gondola Cable Car

Drive 1.5 hours through weeping willow lanes and pine forests to Gulmarg (8,690 ft). Board the renowned Gulmarg Gondola cable car up to Kongdoori. Enjoy snow sports, ATV rides, and sweeping views of Pir Panjal mountain range. Check in to your alpine resort and stroll across Gulmarg golf course.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Gulmarg Alpine Resort
DAY 3

Gulmarg to Pahalgam (Valley of Shepherds) via Saffron Fields

Morning scenic drive to Pahalgam (7,200 ft). Drive past the legendary saffron fields of Pampore and historic Awantipora ruins. In Pahalgam, listen to the roaring turquoise Lidder River. Explore local pine trails, stroll through Pahalgam market, and check into your valley hotel.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Pahalgam Riverside Hotel
DAY 4

Pahalgam Betaab & Aru Valley Tour – Return to Srinagar

Visit picturesque Betaab Valley (named after the Bollywood classic) and Aru Valley. Enjoy pony rides to Baisaran (Mini Switzerland). In the afternoon, drive back to Srinagar and visit the majestic Nishat and Shalimar Mughal Gardens built by Emperor Jahangir.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Srinagar Boutique Hotel
DAY 5

Excursion to Sonmarg Meadow of Gold & Thajiwas Glacier

Day trip to Sonmarg (8,950 ft) along the Sindh River valley surrounded by towering jagged peaks. Take a scenic pony or trek to Thajiwas Glacier where snow remains year-round. Sample fresh river trout or local delicacies before returning to Srinagar.

🍽️ Breakfast & Dinner 🏨 Srinagar Boutique Hotel
DAY 6

Srinagar Lal Chowk Shopping & Airport Departure

After breakfast, shop for authentic Kashmiri pashmina shawls, saffron, and dry fruits. Chauffeur transfer to Srinagar Airport for your onward journey with unforgettable memories of Jannat-e-Kashmir.

🍽️ Breakfast Included ✈️ Flight Departure
