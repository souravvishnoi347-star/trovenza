10-Day Complete Roadmap

Char Dham 2026 Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Haridwar / Dehradun to Barkot (Yamunotri Base)

Pickup in Innova Crysta, scenic drive via Mussoorie and Kempty Falls. Overnight stay at Barkot resort.

Day 2: Barkot to Yamunotri Dham Darshan & Return

Trek/Pony to Yamunotri Temple, Holy bath in Surya Kund, Divya Shila pooja. Return to Barkot resort.

Day 3: Barkot to Uttarkashi (Kashi Vishwanath Mandir)

Drive along Bhagirathi river, visit ancient Kashi Vishwanath temple & Shakti temple. Stay at Uttarkashi.

Day 4: Uttarkashi to Gangotri Dham Darshan & Return

Drive through breathtaking Harsil valley, Ganga snan, Gangotri Mata pooja. Return to Uttarkashi.

Day 5: Uttarkashi to Guptkashi / Phata

Scenic Mandakini river drive, registration check, evening visit to Ardh Narishwar temple. Stay at Guptkashi.

Day 6: Guptkashi to Kedarnath Sanctum (Heli / Trek)

Helicopter shuttle or escorted trek to Kedarnath ji. Evening divine Shiva Aarti. Overnight stay in Kedarnath.

Day 7: Kedarnath Morning Darshan & Return to Guptkashi

Morning Maha Pooja at Kedarnath, fly down to helipad, rest at luxury hillside resort.

Day 8: Guptkashi to Sri Badrinath Dham

Drive through Chopta & Joshimath, check-in near Badrinath temple, attend evening Swarna Aarti.

Day 9: Badrinath Darshan & Mana Village to Rudraprayag

Tapt Kund snan, Badrinath Darshan, Mana Village (India's first village), drive to Rudraprayag.

Day 10: Rudraprayag to Rishikesh / Haridwar Drop

Visit Devprayag Sangam (Bhagirathi + Alaknanda), drop at Haridwar railway station or Dehradun airport.
